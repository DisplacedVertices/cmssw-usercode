gluinos: https://twiki.cern.ch/twiki/bin/view/LHCPhysics/SUSYCrossSections13TeVgluglu (NNLO_approx+NNLL, PDF4LHC21)
stops: https://twiki.cern.ch/twiki/bin/view/LHCPhysics/SUSYCrossSections13TeVstopsbottom (NNLO_approx+NNLL, PDF4LHC21)

Also note for neutralinos that only the N2N1 process and Higgsinos are considered, since this process doesn't exist for winos/binos, and the other processes would include charginos (which our signal samples do not include).
